# M365 Threat Intelligence Report — September 2026

**Report Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Target Period:** September 2026  
**Analyst:** Delphi — Automated Threat Intelligence Collection  
**Audience:** Security Architects · SOC Leads · Cyber Leadership

## Executive Summary

- **AI-powered PhaaS ecosystem targeting M365 at industrial scale**: EvilTokens (Storm-2992) was dismantled by Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit on September 22 after compromising 12,000+ inboxes across 10,000+ organizations; device code phishing attack volume surged 1,380% since H2 2025, with AI-generated lures now achieving ~54% click-through rates versus ~12% for traditional campaigns. The platform model — subscription pricing, customer support, dashboards, and automated token harvesting — signals that MFA-bypass PhaaS is now a mature commercial industry.

- **Microsoft's passkey rollout weaponized as a social engineering pretext**: Storm-3121 (ShinyHunters), Storm-3032 (Helix), and the PREY-0058 cluster are all converging on the same lure: impersonating corporate IT helpdesks to claim a passkey, MFA, or SSO update is urgently required. Victims are directed to AiTM phishing flows or device code auth pages, yielding session tokens and unauthorized MFA device registrations — without any credential theft. The very security improvement Microsoft is rolling out is being turned against users during the transition window.

- **Non-human identity gap exposed at scale**: Two separate September campaigns — TeamFiltration's default-password spray across 28 tenants and Storm-3168's agentic Azure destruction via a leaked GitHub service principal — demonstrate that machine identities (service accounts, service principals) without MFA or secrets rotation are the new phishable perimeter. Forgotten functional accounts with default credentials are a systemic risk across every tenant surveyed.

- **BYOD and personal-device abuse bypass enterprise controls**: Storm-3032 and Storm-3121 are calling and texting corporate executives and admins on their personal phones, deliberately circumventing corporate endpoint controls and authentication policies. Once an employee authenticates on a personal device, attackers pivot through Microsoft Graph to enumerate and exfiltrate data at scale — ShinyHunters and Falcon extortion groups serve as downstream monetization partners.

- **First documented agentic ransomware operation against Azure**: Storm-3168 (JADEPUFFER) executed 150+ destructive Azure operations in a 7-minute automated burst using credentials leaked in GitHub issue history — wiping storage accounts, databases, key vaults, and backup recovery locks before human incident response could engage. This represents a qualitative shift: AI-driven attacks can now outpace SOC response timelines by design, requiring automated defensive countermeasures at equivalent speed.
